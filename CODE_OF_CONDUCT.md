# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in AgentPlan welcoming, respectful and safe for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or language.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, precise and considerate.
- Assume good intent while taking security concerns seriously.
- Explain trade-offs with evidence and welcome correction.
- Respect privacy and do not share credentials, private customer data or exploit details publicly.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on the work, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, malicious disclosure of secrets and advocating harm are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the security or repository contact channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation or take other action appropriate to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from common open-source community standards and is governed by the same principles as the Apache License project community norms.
